5-minute brain-break games for team breaks and meeting icebreakers

A short list of free browser games for sanctioned team breaks and icebreakers. Session lengths are estimates unless a game's own page states one. Each card links to the game's own page or its official host.

Lichess Daily Puzzle (plus puzzle training)

Owner: Lichess (non-profit; free and open source)

  • Session: 1-5 min per puzzle (estimate)
  • Solo

A single chess puzzle is a clean 2-minute break and works as a shared "solve today's puzzle" ritual for a team channel.

Open on lichess.org

Quick, Draw!

Owner: Google (built with Google Creative Lab and Data Arts Team)

  • Session: about 2-3 min per game (estimate)
  • Solo; works as a shared-screen group game

Fast and funny on a shared screen: the team watches an AI try to guess one person's doodles. Each drawing must be done in under 20 seconds.

Note: drawings may be added to Google's public research dataset (stated on the game's page). Don't draw anything personal or confidential.

Open on quickdraw.withgoogle.com

WorldGuessr (single player)

Owner: independent developer (GitHub handle codergautam)

  • Session: about 5 min per game (estimate)
  • Solo; multi via private party

A free geography guessing game (not affiliated with GeoGuessr) that works as a 5-minute group challenge with everyone shouting guesses.

Play single player, or a private party with colleagues only: parties have chat. Ads fund the site.

Open on worldguessr.com

The classic falling-block puzzle (official free web version)

Owner: Tetris Holding (licensed to The Tetris Company)

  • Session: 2-5 min per game (estimate)
  • Solo

The classic everyone already knows; zero explanation needed for a short break.

Open on play.tetris.com

Master Chess

Owner unverified, hosted on Poki (developer credited by Poki: Codethislab)

  • Session: 5-15 min per game (estimate)
  • Solo, or 2 players on the same device

Chess against the computer or a colleague at the same desk.

The hosting page shows ads.

Open on poki.com

Blocky Blast Puzzle

Owner unverified, hosted on Poki (developer credited by Poki: OPlay Games)

  • Session: 3-10 min per round (estimate)
  • Solo

A calm block-placement puzzle that is easy to stop at any moment.

The revive option requires watching an ad.

Open on poki.com

Drift Boss

Owner unverified, hosted on Poki (developer credited by Poki: MarketJS)

  • Session: 1-3 min per run (estimate)
  • Solo

One-button skill game with a high score that suits a quick team "beat my score" challenge.

Open on poki.com

The MachinEGG

Owner: JC - QuantumGames (on itch.io)

  • Session: the game's page lists "About a half-hour"; idle game, runs in a background tab, can be paused any time
  • Solo

A relaxed idle clicker about eggs that can run in a tab and be checked during short breaks.

Open on itch.io

The Evolution of Trust

Owner: Nicky Case

  • Longer workshop pick
  • Session: about 30 min (estimate)
  • Solo

An interactive game-theory explainer about cooperation and trust that makes a strong discussion starter for a team session.

Not a 5-minute break: plan it as a longer session or workshop.

Open on ncase.me
